CIRCULAR: Information Security & Outsourcing Controls (Example)

1. Third-party / outsourcing engagements that process customer or confidential data must have:
   - Signed contract with security obligations, audit rights, and breach notification SLAs.
   - Data Processing Addendum when personal data is processed.
   - Defined sub-processor approval and disclosure.

2. Cloud hosting requires:
   - Documented data residency evaluation.
   - Encryption of data at rest and in transit.
   - IAM controls using least privilege and MFA.

3. Penetration testing:
   - Must be completed prior to go-live for internet-exposed applications.
   - Remediation must be tracked with evidence.
