POLICY: Data Protection & Privacy Standard (Example)

A. Personal data processing requires:
   - Lawful basis/consent where applicable.
   - Data minimization and purpose limitation.
   - Retention schedule aligned to regulatory requirements.

B. Cross-border transfers:
   - Require approved transfer mechanism and vendor DPA.
   - Must document country of processing and storage.

C. Logging:
   - Avoid logging full PII in application logs.
   - Implement access monitoring for sensitive data.
