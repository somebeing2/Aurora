POLICY: AML/KYC Systems Change Standard (Example)

1. Any change impacting KYC capture, screening, transaction monitoring, or onboarding approvals must:
   - Be reviewed by Financial Crime Compliance.
   - Maintain audit trails for decisions and overrides.
   - Not reduce detection coverage without documented compensating controls.

2. Vendor solutions used in KYC flows require:
   - Model validation where automated decisioning is used.
   - Ongoing monitoring for false positives/negatives.
